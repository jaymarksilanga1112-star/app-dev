# Coach Carter (2005)
**Coach Carter** is an inspiring true-story sports drama about discipline, responsibility, and believing in young people.
> "I came to teach success. And success starts with hard work, dedication, and respect."  
> — Coach Ken Carter
## Why It’s Powerful
- Strong performances, especially from Samuel L. Jackson
- Important message about education over sports
- Motivational and emotional story
- Based on real events
---
## Main Characters
1. **Coach Ken Carter** - Strict but caring basketball coach (Samuel L. Jackson)
2. **Kenyon Stone** - Talented player dealing with pressure (Rob Brown)
3. **Timo Cruz** - Troubled player looking for direction (Rick Gonzalez)
4. **Kyra** - Kenyon’s girlfriend (Ashanti)
## Key Themes
- Discipline and accountability
- The importance of education
- Teamwork and respect
- Second chances
## Quick Facts
- Based on the real-life story of Coach Ken Carter
- Released in 2005
- Features a strong soundtrack
- Remains a popular motivational movie
The scene where Coach Carter locks the gym and forces the players to focus on their grades is unforgettable.
